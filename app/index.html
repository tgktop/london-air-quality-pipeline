CleanAir London Intelligence Hub

A purpose-built ELT pipeline dashboard monitoring London Air Quality Network (LAQN) telemetry to evaluate urban pollution exposure and spatial network density.

Executive Policy Summary

With nearly half of the network concentrated on roadside telemetry, monitoring infrastructure is heavily weighted toward high-traffic commuter exposure. This enables city planners to instantly evaluate pedestrian risk and prioritise pollution mitigation zones across London's 33 boroughs.
